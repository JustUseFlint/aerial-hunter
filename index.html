(Aerial Hunter)

Aerial Hunter is a drone search service based in Parke County, Indiana. A certified Part 107 pilot flies a DJI Matrice 4T thermal drone over fields, timber and creek bottoms to find what’s hard to see from the ground: a deer that ran after the shot, a dog that slipped its collar, a herd spread across back pasture.

The gear

Built for search. Not for selfies.

The DJI Matrice 4T is an enterprise drone made for search and public-safety work: a radiometric thermal camera, wide and zoom cameras, a laser rangefinder and a near-infrared light, all on one compact airframe.

  • Thermal640 × 512
  • ZoomUp to 168 mm equiv.
  • Rangefinder1,800 m
  • NIR light100 m
