See theunseen.

(How thermal works)

Everything gives off heat. A thermal camera doesn’t need light; it reads the difference in temperature between things. A deer, a dog or a cow is warmer than the leaves and soil around it, so it glows. The bigger the difference, the clearer the picture, which is why cool, still nights are often the best time to search.

Part 107 certified

Licensed to fly for hire.

Part 107 is the FAA rulebook for commercial drone work. A certified remote pilot has passed the FAA’s aeronautical knowledge test and keeps current with recurrent training. That covers airspace, weather, night operations and safety, all of which matter when you’re flying over timber after dark.

  • FAA-certified remote pilot
  • Night flights with anti-collision lighting
  • Airspace checked before every flight
