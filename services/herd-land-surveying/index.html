(The problem)

Some questions are easier to answer from 300 feet up. How many head are in the back eighty? Where did that fence go down? Did the creek take part of the bank? We fly a set pattern over your ground, with regular and thermal cameras, and hand you the answers along with the photos and video.

How a search works

01 / 05

The call

Tell us roughly how many acres, what is on the ground, and what you need: a count, a fence check, a stray search or just good aerial footage.

02 / 05

The plan

We agree on boundaries and timing. Thermal headcounts work best in cool air, early in the morning or after dark, when animals stand out clearly.

03 / 05

The flight

Photo, video and thermal passes over pasture, timber and water, flown in a set pattern so nothing gets counted twice.

04 / 05

The review

We go through the footage for counts, strays, fence breaks, washouts and anything else worth a closer look.
