How every search runs

Call. Plan. Fly. Find.

  1. Call

    Tell us what you’ve lost and where. We ask the right questions and give you an honest read.

  2. Plan

    We check airspace, weather and permission, and plan a search pattern around the last known spot.

  3. Fly

    The thermal drone sweeps the area in a methodical grid, day or night.

  4. Find

    Signatures are confirmed on the zoom camera, then we guide you straight to them.
